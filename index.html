The atelier

How a Hawksman suit is made

Masterfully crafted
  1. i.

    Consultation

    We talk through the occasion, how you like to wear a suit, and look through cloth bunches together to settle the style.

    First visit
  2. ii.

    Measure & draft

    Twenty-seven measurements and notes on posture. Your paper pattern is drafted from them, then chalked and cut into cloth.

    Same visit, then the workroom
  3. iii.

    Fittings

    The garment is tried on while still open at the seams, so the balance, drape and length can be corrected on you.

    One to three fittings
  4. iv.

    Finishing

    Buttonholes, pressing and a final check in the mirror. Your pattern stays on file for every future order.

    Ready to collect

Questions

Before your first visit

How long does a bespoke suit take?

Usually a few weeks from the first measurement, depending on the garment and the number of fittings. If you have a wedding or event date, tell us at the first visit and we will plan the fittings around it.

How many fittings will I need?

A suit normally has one to three fittings. Shirts and trousers usually need fewer. Each fitting is how we correct balance and drape on you rather than on paper.

Can I bring my own cloth?

Yes. Bring it to the consultation and we will check it is suitable for the garment you have in mind.

Do you dress wedding parties?

Yes. We make suits for grooms and coordinated sets for groomsmen and family. Start early so everyone's fittings can be scheduled before the date.

I live outside Kozhikode. Can I still order?

Ask about a home visit or a video consultation. Your pattern is kept on file, so reorders do not need a new measurement.
